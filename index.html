Page Setup Studio

Any page size and margins in Google Docs, in a few clicks.

What it does

  • Choose from paper, book, card, envelope and slide sizes, or type your own size in inches, millimetres, centimetres or points.
  • Set portrait or landscape, swap width and height, and set top, bottom, left and right margins plus a gutter.
  • Use margin presets, including book margins based on the number of pages, or let it fit margins to the page.
  • Change the current tab or every tab at once, and undo the last 10 changes.
  • Save your favourite sizes and use them in any document.

How to use it

After installing, open a document and choose Extensions → Page Setup Studio → Open page setup.

Your data

The add-on only reads and changes the page size and margins of the document you have open. It never reads, stores or sends your text or images. See the privacy policy.
