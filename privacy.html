Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 (drop caps added)

Page Setup Studio ("the add-on") is a Google Docs add-on made by Ngwana Joshua. This policy explains what the add-on can access and what it does with it.

What the add-on accesses

  • The document you have open, only while you use the add-on (Google permission documents.currentonly). It reads and changes the page size and the page margins, and it reads the names of the document's tabs so you can change one tab or all of them.
  • Drop caps (only when you use that tool): the add-on looks for very short paragraphs set in a large font (a chapter's big first letter) and reads the font, size and colour of the paragraph after it. When you choose to fix them, it replaces each big letter with a small picture of the same letter, drawn inside your browser, and can put the original letters back.
  • A panel in Google Docs (permission script.container.ui), to show the add-on's controls.

The add-on does not copy, store or send the text, images or other content of your documents anywhere. Everything happens inside Google Docs and your browser.

What the add-on stores

  • Undo history: the previous page sizes and margins of the document, and for drop caps the original letter and its formatting, kept inside that document's add-on storage so you can undo a change.
  • Your saved sizes: the names and numbers of sizes you choose to save, kept in your own Google account's add-on storage.

This information is stored by Google as part of Google Apps Script. The developer cannot see it.

Sharing

The add-on does not send any data to the developer or to anyone else. It has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Removing your data

Uninstall the add-on from Google Docs (Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons) and remove its access in your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps with account access). Saved sizes and undo history are no longer used after that.

Google API Services user data policy

The add-on's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
